
# Point, Ask, Done.
### Turn the real world into actionable cards with VePulse.

**Built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026**

Powered by **Meta Model API · SAM 3.1 · Muse Spark**

---

## 🚀 The Idea

**Point your camera. Get a card. Take action.**

Useful information is everywhere in the physical world, but it isn't always easy to use.

An event poster has a date and location. A restaurant menu has a dish worth remembering. A business card has someone you want to connect with.

Today, capturing that information usually means taking a photo, searching for details, copying text, or manually creating something yourself.

**Point, Ask, Done changes that.**

Inside VePulse, simply point your camera at a poster, menu, or business card. AI understands what you're looking at and transforms it into a structured, interactive card.

No copying. No searching. No unnecessary steps.

**From something you see to something you can do.**

---

## ✨ How It Works

The experience is designed around one simple flow:

**📷 Point → 🧠 Understand → 🪄 Create → ⚡ Act**

1. **Point** — Open the camera inside the VePulse demo and capture a poster, menu, or business card.
2. **Detect** — SAM 3.1 identifies the relevant object in the image.
3. **Understand** — Muse Spark interprets the content and organizes it into structured information.
4. **Create** — VePulse transforms that information into a native, interactive card.
5. **Act** — Save, share, connect, or take the next step directly from the card.

The result isn't another chatbot response.

**It's a useful interface built from the real world.**

## 🃏 Three Objects. Three Experiences.

### 📅 1. Event Card

**See an event poster? Turn it into a plan.**

Point your camera at a concert poster, community event announcement, or local gathering.

VePulse creates an event card containing:

- Event name
- Date and time
- Location

**Available actions:**
- 📅 Add to Calendar
- 📍 Get Directions
- 🔗 Share Event
- 💬 Talk About It

### 🍽️ 2. Dish Card

**See something delicious? Remember it.**

Point your camera at a restaurant menu and select a dish.

VePulse creates a dish card containing:

- Dish name
- Price
- Short description

**Available actions:**
- 🔖 Save Dish
- 🔗 Share Dish

### 👤 3. Person Card

**Receive a business card? Make a connection.**

Point your camera at a business card someone has shared with you.

VePulse creates a person card containing:

- Person's name
- Job title
- Company

**Available actions:**
- ➕ Follow
- 💬 Message

**Privacy by design:** Person Cards are created from voluntarily shared business cards. This feature does not use facial recognition or face search.

This project brings together Meta's AI capabilities and VePulse's native social experience.

|---|---|
| **Meta Model API** | Access to Meta's AI models |
| **SAM 3.1** | Identifying relevant objects and regions within images |

Instead of using AI only to answer questions, we're exploring how AI can create the right interface for what someone is looking at.

**The camera becomes the starting point. The card becomes the interface.**
